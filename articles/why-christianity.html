Why Christianity?

To see why Christianity is valid, there are several questions to be addressed:

  • Was Jesus historically a real person?
  • If he was real, did he die by crucifixion?
  • If he was crucified, did he come back to life?
  • If he rose from the dead, what does that imply?
  • If he claims to be God, does God exist?
  • If God exists, why Christianity’s God rather than other religions?
  • If the Christian God is real, and Jesus claims to be God, why did God become human?
  • Why would we trust the transmission of Christian teachings over 2,000 years?
  • How did Christianity develop from the time of Pentecost to present day?
