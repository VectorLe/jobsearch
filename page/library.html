All the physical books I own. I have not read all of them (actually, maybe a majority •︵•). As Nassim Taleb puts it:

The library should contain as much of what you do not know as your financial means … allow you to put there. You will accumulate more knowledge and more books as you grow older, and the growing number of unread books on the shelves will look at you menacingly. Indeed, the more you know, the larger the rows of unread books. Let us call this collection of unread books an antilibrary.

There's also a Japanese term to describe the something similar:

Tsundoku (積ん読) is the phenomenon of acquiring reading materials but letting them pile up in a home without reading them.

Is this cope? Generally, I read a lot better/faster when there's less friction between me being interested and actually reading. The reason why I have a lot of books that I haven't read yet is cause I like being able to drop whatever I'm currently reading to switch to something that I want to read at a particular time.
