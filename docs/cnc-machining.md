# CNC machining: career and sourcing notes

Reviewed November 16, 2026. This page documents where facts came from, what has not been verified, and why CNC is relevant to a hands-on SGV career change. No program is presented as an open job.

## Job levels and daily work

- **CNC operator or trainee:** Load material, execute established programs, take measurements and maintain a machine. Often a more accessible starting point, but not all employers train beginners.
- **CNC setup machinist:** Choose tools, fixtures and feeds; set offsets and verify first parts. Often needs previous machine shop experience.
- **CNC programmer:** CAD/CAM toolpaths, programs and optimization. Usually more screen-based and often requires prior machining experience.
- **Quality inspector:** Inspect dimensions and tolerances and document results. Less machine setup, but still technical and on-site.

A computer engineering background may help with geometry, controls and programming concepts, but does not substitute for machining experience. Shop work can involve standing, rotating shifts, noise, coolant, moving equipment and strict safety procedures.
