The broader `data/apprenticeships.json` catalog also lists welding, reinforcing ironwork, industrial pipefitting, heavy equipment, glaziers, carpenters and open-shop training as alternatives. Qualifications, pay and intake status are labeled as requiring sponsor confirmation when no current information was published.

For a practical career transition, prioritize earning while learning, realistic commute, manual work demands, dues or fees, mathematics requirements, medical or safety qualifications, expected application delay and the likelihood of training leading to actual employment. Never use a training-program wage as a guarantee of present job pay.
